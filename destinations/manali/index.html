Taxi to Manali

We drive Dharamshala to Manali every week. The route runs through Palampur, Baijnath, Mandi and the Kullu valley along the Beas river.

Leave Dharamshala early to reach Manali in daylight. We stop for breakfast and lunch on the way, and can break the journey at Kullu for river rafting or Naggar.

In Manali we can stay on for local sightseeing to Solang Valley and the Atal Tunnel. Rohtang Pass needs a separate vehicle permit; ask us when you book and we will tell you what is possible on your dates.

Highlights

  • Hadimba Devi temple and Old Manali
  • Solang Valley
  • Atal Tunnel and Sissu
  • Kullu and Naggar castle on the way

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to drive to Manali in winter?

Yes, with an experienced hill driver and the right car. Snow can close roads above Manali, so keep a day spare in your plan.

Can I book one way only?

Yes. We do one-way drops from Dharamshala, Gaggal airport or Pathankot to Manali.
