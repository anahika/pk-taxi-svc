Taxi to Spiti Valley

Spiti is a high-altitude desert valley on the Tibetan border. It is one of the most beautiful drives in India, and one of the toughest, so the driver matters.

We plan Spiti as a full trip, not a drop. A typical loop is 6 to 8 days: Dharamshala to Manali, over the Atal Tunnel and Kunzum Pass to Kaza, then back the same way or out through Kinnaur.

Kaza is above 3,600 m. Plan rest days, drink plenty of water and avoid rushing over the passes. Our drivers know the river crossings and fuel stops, which are few and far apart.

Highlights

  • Key Monastery and Kibber village
  • Chandratal lake
  • Langza, Hikkim and Komic villages
  • Dhankar Monastery and Tabo

Frequently asked questions

When is the Manali–Kaza road open?

Usually from June to October. Exact dates depend on snow on Kunzum Pass each year.

Do you go to Chandratal?

Yes, on the Manali–Kaza route, road conditions permitting.

Distance
Manali to Kaza ~200 km
Drive time
Two days from Dharamshala with a night in Manali
Best time to go
June–October, when Kunzum Pass is open

Distances and times are approximate and depend on road and weather.
