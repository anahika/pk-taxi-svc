Taxi to Dharamshala & McLeodganj

Dharamshala is our home base. We pick you up from Gaggal (Kangra) airport, Pathankot railway station or the bus stand and take you anywhere in town, up to McLeodganj, or out on day trips.

Most of our customers start their Himachal trip in Dharamshala. A local sightseeing day usually covers McLeodganj, Bhagsu Nag, Naddi and the cricket stadium, and takes 5 to 7 hours with stops.

The roads between lower Dharamshala and McLeodganj are narrow and steep, and parking is limited in season. Our drivers know where to drop you and where to wait, so you spend your time sightseeing instead of looking for a spot.

For early flights from Gaggal or late trains at Pathankot, book ahead and we will be there on time, day or night.

Highlights

  • McLeodganj and the Tsuglagkhang (Dalai Lama Temple) complex
  • Bhagsu Nag temple and waterfall
  • Naddi viewpoint for sunset over the Dhauladhar
  • HPCA cricket stadium
  • Dharamkot and Galu, the start of the Triund trek

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from Gaggal airport to McLeodganj?

About 45 minutes to an hour, depending on traffic in Dharamshala town.

Do you pick up from Pathankot railway station at night?

Yes. We run 24/7. Share your train number and we will track any delay.
