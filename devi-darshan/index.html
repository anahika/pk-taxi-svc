Kangra Devi darshan by taxi

Visit the four Devi temples of the Kangra valley in one day, in a comfortable car with a driver who knows the temple routes and parking.

The route

  1. Stop 1

    Chamunda Devi

    On the Baner river, ~15 km from Dharamshala.

  2. Stop 2

    Brajeshwari Devi

    In Kangra town, one of the Shakti Peeths.

  3. Stop 3

    Baglamukhi

    At Bankhandi, between Kangra and Jwalamukhi.

  4. Stop 4

    Jwala Ji

    Famous for its eternal flames, ~55 km from Dharamshala.

Distances are approximate. We adjust the order to avoid crowds on festival days.

Frequently asked questions

Can all four Devi temples be covered in one day?

Yes. Starting early from Dharamshala, Chamunda, Brajeshwari, Baglamukhi and Jwala Ji fit into one full day of about 9 to 11 hours, depending on queues at the temples.

Can you add Chintpurni or Naina Devi?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will plan a two-day circuit with an overnight stop.

Do you pick up from Gaggal airport or Pathankot for the darshan?

Yes. We can start the circuit straight from the airport or railway station.
